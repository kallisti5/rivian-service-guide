# Rivian known problems and solutions

## R1 Gen 1

> Issues and defects encountered on an *early* launch edition R1T (vin < 1000)

### Hardware

* *Description:* "Tinging noise while driving at low speeds"
  * Pop off the center plastic hub of each wheel, check for a loose bolt.
  * This bolt backs out on early R1 vehicles and pulls the hub to the drive shaft.
  * Rivian had a recall to address it. The recall involves better solutions to prevent backout.
* *Description:* "Loud suspension 'creak' adjusting suspension or going over minor bumps"
  * Known defect. Make sure the issue is occurring, and record video of noise.
  * Rivian service will generally replace several suspension components to address.
  * Related to excessive wear of 
* *Description:* "Musty smell in cabin.  Wet front carpet"
  * A/C condensation pan leak.  A/C condensation pan develops a defect which causes condensation
    to drain into cabin.
  * Rivian known defect. Service will repair under warranty.
* *Description:* "Continuous random DCFC session payment errors, failed DCFC sessions"
  * Unique issue, likely limited to some *early* R1 vehicles.
  * Extremely difficult to get Rivian Service to identify and acknowledge.
  * Eventual solution (after 4+ visits reporting issue with tens of videos of it happening)
    was replacement of the Central Gateway Module tagged as "Goodwill provided for customer satisfaction".
  * Replacement of Central Gateway Module resolved all DCFC communication / payment issues.

### Software

* *Description:* "All warning lights suddenly on during operating, vehicle forces you to pull over"
  * Resolution: Pull over to a safe location, perform a full vehicle reset (hold left most button on
    steering wheel, and emergency flashers)
  * Cause: Unknown, potentially related to failing Central Gateway Module?
